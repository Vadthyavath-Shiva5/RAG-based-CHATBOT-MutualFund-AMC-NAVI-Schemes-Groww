Navi AMC (formerly Essel mutual fund) is founded by Sachin Bansal (co-founder and ex-Chairman of Flipkart) and Ankit Agarwal (ex-banker with Deutsche Bank and Bank of America). The AMC's sponsor is Anmol Como Broking Private Limited and it is registered with the Securities & Exchange Board of India (SEBI) under the same name. The investment manager of Navi Mutual Funds is Navi AMC Limited and the trustee to Navi AMC Limited is Navi Trustee Limited. Not only confined to Navi AMC, Navi also offers a plethora of financial services and products including Personal Loans, Home Loans, vehicle Loans (two wheeler), SME Business Loans and also General Insurance.

Navi AMC was started in December 2018 with a mission to make financial services hassle-free, affordable and simply accessible. It leverages a technology-driven and customer-centric approach to establish products in the financial services space. In January 2021, Navi acquired Essel Finance Mutual Fund and renamed it to Navi.

How can you invest in Navi Mutual Funds?

Follow the steps mentioned below to invest in Navi Mutual Fund online:

Step 1. Log-in to your Groww account or register to create a new one.

Step 2. Upload your identity proof documents - Voter ID, Aadhaar, PAN, Driving License, Passport, Central or State Government ID card, etc.

Step 3. Upload your address proof documents - any identity KYC document with your permanent address, etc.

Step 4. Select the period you want to invest in.

Step 5. Choose your preferred risk level - low, mid, or high.

Step 6. Select the Navi Mutual Fund you want to invest in.

Step 7. Click on “Invest One Time” if you want to invest a lump sum amount; click “Start SIP” if you want to invest via SIP.

Your Groww account will reflect the Navi Mutual Fund in India you have invested in within 3 to 4 working days.

FAQs on Navi Mutual Fund

How to Start Navi Mutual Fund SIP Online?

One can start investing in Navi Mutual Fund online through Groww without any hassles. Alternatively, they can also invest through the respective fund houses’ website. One can also make use of any other online platform or mobile application to invest in Navi Mutual Funds.

How to Calculate Navi Mutual Fund SIP?

To calculate Navi Mutual Fund SIP, one needs to take into account certain things such as:

  1. the investment amount
  2. the tenure of SIP
  3. the number of your Navi Mutual Fund SIPs (if there's any) already paid
  4. expected interest rate

How to redeem Navi Mutual Fund?

To redeem Navi Mutual Funds through offline mode, one can visit the nearest fund house and submit a form. Alternatively, an investor can also visit the official website and redeem the investment by signing in with the folio number. They can also withdraw their Navi Mutual Fund investments from any online portal where they have invested.

How to increase SIP amount in Navi Mutual Fund?

An Navi Mutual Fund sip amount can be increased with the help of Step-up or top-up SIPs. Earlier, this facility was provided by very few fund houses. Now, however, most of them offer it but it is advisable to check with the fund house first. If the facility is available, it is advisable to check with Step-up Calculator to calculate the SIP Amount and then take any decision.

How to stop a SIP in an Navi Mutual Fund online?

Yes, you can stop your SIP online at any time by just placing a Cancel SIP request. To stop or cancel your SIP, you can either log in to the Navi Mutual Funds website with a folio number or contact your agent or check the steps on the online investment platform where you have invested.
